## Description

    300 million years ago, dragonflies had wings as long as your arm, a millipede grew longer than a car, and the forests that became our coal were still standing. There were no dinosaurs, no birds, no flowers and no grass.

    In this paper-cut story we walk through the last stretch of the Carboniferous: the scale-tree swamps, the oxygen-rich air, the giant arthropods, the crocodile-sized amphibians, the wildfires, and what it would take for a modern human to get by there.
