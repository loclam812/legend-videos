## Description

    66 million years ago, an asteroid about 10 km wide struck a shallow sea in what is now Mexico. A paper-cut look at the first ten minutes: the fireball, the crater, a brief 4.5 km wall of water, and seismic waves that shook the ground thousands of kilometres away.

    How this video is made
    The paper layers are generated with a local AI image model (FLUX.2 klein) and animated by code. The narration is a synthetic voice (Kokoro). The script and its figures were written and checked by the channel.
